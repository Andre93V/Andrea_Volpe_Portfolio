## What I Do

I specialize in Supply Chain and Operations—the critical intersection where strategic planning meets the unpredictable reality of the shop floor. With a foundation in precision manufacturing for the automotive and aerospace sectors, I have built my career in environments where "good enough" is never an option and precision is a baseline requirement.
Over the past nine years, I have progressed from lean improvement and procurement to leading end-to-end Supply Chain operations. My track record includes managing a €5M automation programme, navigating a complex ERP migration during a corporate transition, and maintaining an OTIF level above 95% during a high-stakes plant transfer.
I bridge the gap between technical data and human processes. Whether I’m leveraging Excel, Power BI, or Python to extract insights, or building workflows that actually work for the people using them. I focus on maintaining a calm and analytical approach when operations become volatile—because sometimes they do.
